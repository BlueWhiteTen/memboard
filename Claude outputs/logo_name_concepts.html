10 marks, 10 names

Each card pairs one logo idea with one name suggestion, but none of it is locked together — mix and match freely. Every mark is drawn as a single-line ink icon (not a filled color block), so it reads the same on parchment or on a night sky without a separate dark-mode version.

01 / CONSTELLATION
Kindred
A loose cluster of points, joined by thin lines — the people you keep close, mapped rather than listed.
02 / ATLAS RULE
Atlas
A globe's graticule lines, drawn like an old map — a chart of everywhere a memory happened.
03 / WAYMARK
Waypoint
A single map pin with a star for its dot — the spots worth marking down as you go.
04 / CAIRN
Cairn
Stones stacked one at a time — small markers left behind, the way memories get added one by one.
05 / KEEP
Keepsake
A simple bookmark shape — the plainest possible way to say "this is worth holding on to."
06 / TAPESTRY
Tapestry
Two threads crossing back and forth — many small strands from different people, woven into one record.
07 / ALMANAC
Almanac
A page torn from a dated record book — a running account of a life, kept year over year.
08 / HEARTH
Fireside
A single flame — the gathering-place feeling, the reason people come back to tell each other things.
09 / MOSAIC
Mosaic
The current four-square mark, redrawn as an outline instead of a filled block — familiar, but it survives dark mode.
10 / LANTERN
Lantern
A small lantern, banded like an old sea-lantern — something to carry the past forward by, one memory lighting the next.
This is a first pass to react to, not a shortlist — say which marks or names you want refined, combined, or thrown out, and I'll take the next round further in that direction. Nothing here touches the live app yet.
